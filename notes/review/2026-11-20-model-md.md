## Findings

### 1. G9 now explains the opposite of the miss it reports (major for Methods)
MODEL.md 626-631: batch 1 has "too few" goats past 4 years (0.14 of the slaughtered, 0.11 of all herd deaths). The
item then keeps the old explanation: "Does keep their worth to age 9 because nothing in the model makes an older doe
kid less ... a herder has no observed reason to cull her early. Candidate: age-declining fertility". That explains too
**many** old goats, the pre-batch-1 miss, and adding age-declining fertility would make the batch-1 miss worse. The
batch-1 shortfall is probably the males: about 0.69 of slaughtered males die before 2.5 y, and the slaughter F:M is 0.15
(EXPLOG 5113). That is my reading, not measured. **Fix:** split G9 into the old miss with its explanation, and the
batch-1 miss with its cause marked unknown or measured (kill-off by sex past 4 y).

### 2. §10 has no open item for the two mechanisms batch 1's main results rest on (major for Methods)
- **R.** G13 sets "In the batch-1 anchor (exp004, REF) the R gap is −0.63" beside a diagnosis from the old model. It
  does not mention that the spook hazard scales with herd size through herdLoad, which exp007 is now testing (design
  review finding 1). A reader will take the old retention diagnosis as the explanation of −0.63.
- **B.** C57 says the herd-mean credit "is what shrinks herd body size". Nowhere does MODEL.md say that the B gap
  runs from +0.03 to −0.67 with the reference size chosen (EXPLOG 4823-4826, 4873-4877, 4907), or that B does not
  settle by 4000 years.

**Fix:** add open items G15 (the R gap depends on the load rule; exp007 pending) and G16 (B depends on the herder's
reference size; B5 pending). The author's Methods and Limits will be written from §10.

### 3. G13's scope sentence contradicts the next sentence (minor)
MODEL.md 650-651: "every figure in this item is from that model. In the batch-1 anchor (exp004, REF) the R gap is
−0.63." Move the batch-1 figure out of the item (to G15), or say "every figure but this one".

### 4. "Heritable R" as literature-backed (minor)
MODEL.md 589 lists "heritable R" among the literature-backed numbers. The literature supports that tameness and
reactivity are heritable. It does not support the model's heritability of 1, with no expression noise (C20). Say
"R heritable (the model sets heritability 1, C20; the literature gives moderate values)" or drop it from the list.

### 5. Small omissions in C57 and C77 (minor)
- The keep value's last-breeder term (`lastBreederValue`, world.js:1235-1236, 0 by default) is in the code but not in
  the C57 term list. §12 mentions it at 2026-10-27. Add one clause saying it is off.
- When a herd has no goat of a sex at or above creditAge, the herd-mean reference for that sex is 1 (world.js:1149,
  `an[0] ? ... : 1`). That matters for small or newly founded herds. Say so in C57.
- C77 does not give `cropDamage`'s default (0, off); C76 gives `siteGrass`'s.
