## 1. "Match in size, carried by opposite pools": accurate, and one more tool line would say why (moderate)
The paragraph is no stronger than the tables, with one wording change:
- **"Match in size"** should read "do not differ detectably". The intervals are about ±0.08 wide.
- **The coincidence needs explaining.** Two different processes giving the same gap size is the thing to explain.
  One reading the tables already suggest: a closed herd settles at its own value of A and H, whatever its founders. At
  year 400 the founders, drawn from an unsettled wild, already sat near that value, and the wild left them. From a
  settled wild, the herd moves to the same value by itself. Then the gap is herd equilibrium minus wild equilibrium in
  both designs. That would be relaxation in the herd stopped short by selection there (exp009: A and H moved less
  than C in a closed herd).
- **The test.** The absolute window means of herd and wild on A, S, H and W, year-400 arm against late arm, unpaired.
  If the herd's absolute A and H match across designs while its founders differ, that is the reading. scored.txt
  prints changes only; this needs a tool line. I won't compute it from the tables.

**Fix:** reword "match in size" as above. Add the absolute-means line, exploratory, before the paper uses the
"opposite pools" sentence.

## 2. REFlate's "sheltered from human-driven selection" misdescribes S and W (moderate, wording)
REFlate S and W fire the human-driven branch (human-caused change +0.060 and −0.073). The rule's text then says "the
gap is the herd sheltered from human-driven selection on the wild".

But REFlate's wild moves toward its herd:
- S: herd +0.160, wild +0.049;
- W: herd −0.196, wild −0.066.

REFlate is the open arm (captive-sire share 0.073), and its herd-born goats enter the wild. That is the herd pulling
the wild, not the herd sheltered from a selection the wild is under. REFlate doesn't govern, but the sentence is in
the entry.

**Fix:** for REFlate S and W, say "the wild moves toward the herd (herd-born goats entering it). The registered branch
fires, and its 'shelter' wording does not fit". The closed arms' human-caused changes are all under 0.05.

name L.
