## 1. Prediction 2's "non-decreasing" is strict, while the rule calls a fall of 1 noise (minor, reading)
The selftest case 0 3 2 5 7 7 reads "graded" under the rule yet fails prediction 2. That is the faithful reading: the
clarification set the tolerance for the rule's "not monotone", not for the prediction's own word. Keep it. If it
happens, the results entry should say that prediction 2 fails on a fall the rule treats as noise.

## 2. "Median herd in span" is the raw herd over a span defined on the 100-year mean (minor, label)
The span is measured on the smoothed series, and the median is taken over the raw `herded` of the same points
(`wait_span`, the r[2] values). For a long plateau the two agree: LOAD1's medians are 3-6 goats. For a short span just
after 500 they don't. LOAD05 s2 has a span of 9 years with a median herd of 164, because its 100-year mean was still
catching up with a herd already grown. That is the right number for its purpose: it tells a plateau from no herd. It
is not a plateau level when the span is short, though.

**Fix:** label the column "median raw herd (year rows) over the span". The results entry reads it only for spans of
200 years or more.

## 3. "Waited, still" can include a herd that never formed (minor)
The span counts any time under 60, including a herd of 0-3 goats throughout, as at loads 2 and above in exp010. At 0.6
to 0.9 this is unlikely, since LOAD1's spans had medians of 3-6. The per-seed median shows it if it happens. If a
still-waiting seed's median raw herd is under 3, the results entry should say so before calling it a wait. Nothing
needs to change in the tool.
