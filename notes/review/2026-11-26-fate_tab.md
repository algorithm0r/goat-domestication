## On the stock floor (the steward's confirmation, 2026-11-26)
**The choice.** fate_tab applies n ≥ 20 for stock0 to the stock count summed over the phase's Y−1 year ends, in
goat-years (line 19, and `tot` at 90-94). ecc681e says "goats for the stock" without saying per year or pooled.

**The reading is fair.** It is the same summed count by which f0e1d2e b pools R̄₀, so the floor and the mean rest on
one quantity. A per-year floor would be a different rule, and nothing registered asks for one.

Like the other floors, it guards only against near-empty cells (f0e1d2e f). Accepted, and the scored text says so
beside the floor.
