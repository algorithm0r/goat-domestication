## 1. "The filter on the herd's own lineage" claims more than the tables show for A and T (moderate)
**The draft.** "The herd-born stock alone shows the filter on the herd's own lineage", followed by the herd-born losses
−0.8270, −0.7259 and −0.5786.

**The tables.**
- **The spook step barely separates herd-born goats.** In the REF filter table, "R spooked − R walking" for herd-born
  goats is −0.0004 in A, +0.0010 in T and +0.0167 in B. In A and T, a spooked herd-born goat has about the R of a
  walking one.
- **The losses are still large.** The herd-born loss channel carries −0.8270 and −0.7259 over the same two phases.
- **Lost goats sit near walking R.** Lost herd-born goats' R is 0.720 against walking 0.710 in A, and 0.520 against
  0.501 in T.

So in A and T, the herd-born loss channel's size doesn't come from the spook step selecting high-R goats among
walkers. Lost herd-born goats leave above the herd-born stock's mean, but the tool doesn't print that mean by phase,
and it doesn't separate why.

**Candidates, none checked:**
- night straying (lostNight −0.2143 and −0.2084 of the herd-born-alone losses);
- who walks against who stays at camp (the stock includes kept and other-herd goats);
- age or lineage, such as kids of captive mothers.

**Fix:**
- Replace the sentence with "The herd-born stock alone shows its own losses leaving above its mean."
- Give herd-born spooked − walking R by phase (−0.0004, +0.0010, +0.0167, +0.0488), and say that the spook step
  separates herd-born goats by R only from B on.
- Say that what puts lost herd-born goats above the herd-born mean in A and T is not separated by this batch.
