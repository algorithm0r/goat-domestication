## 1. "The cost does not select here" is contradicted in the wild; prediction 7 reads only the herd (major, for the wording)
Branch C's text says the cost does not select. The registered contrast against the demographic control says it does,
in the wild (scored.txt, paired table):
- **DEEP50 − DEEPFLAT50, wild p: −0.052 [−0.070, −0.035]** over 8 seeds. Same demography, gradient on or off.
- **DEEP50 − CTLDEEP, wild p: −0.030 [−0.046, −0.014].**
- **RAND50 − CTLRAND, wild p: −0.025 [−0.040, −0.010].**

Prediction 7's counter cannot see this. The selection pool is herd adults only: `world.js:1492` skips `g.herd < 0`, and
only herd kids are scored. So prediction 7 says the cost does not select within the herd. It says nothing about the
wild.

**When the wild moved.** Most of the wild's response comes before herding.
- Against its own founders over 3201-4000, DEEP50 − DEEPFLAT50 wild change is −0.017 [−0.039, +0.004].
- The founders therefore already carry about −0.035 of the −0.052, by subtracting these two tool lines.
- This fits the reading aid's founders' shift at 0.5 (T +0.113, M −0.092, S −0.052, A −0.047), identical across the
  0.5 arms.

**Inside the closed herd, after founding, the cost adds nothing measurable:**
- herd change of p, DEEP50 − DEEPFLAT50: +0.006 [−0.037, +0.048];
- the mid-parent differential: +0.002 [−0.002, +0.005];
- prediction 2's gap change: +0.021 [−0.024, +0.066].

**My part in this.** In 12e1e47 point 3 I proposed the herd's selection differential as the test of whether the cost
selects. I wrote that a null on it means "the cost does not select". I did not say that the counter reads only the
herd. Branch C's wording comes from there, so this gap is mine.

**Fix (entry and paper):**
- Replace "the cost does not select here" with: "the cost lowers wild p (−0.052 against its demographic control),
  mostly before herding begins. Within closed herds it adds no detectable selection on p (prediction 7), and the herd
  does not move away from its wild under it (predictions 2 and 5)."
- Narrow "the null says nothing about the mechanism". It says that the per-heat cost, as built, acts in the wild and
  not within managed herds. Whether retries in the herd dilute it is still untested, and it remains the registered
  candidate.
- If the paper needs the cause, the counter I asked for in 12e1e47 (conceptions per heat, or does-seasons with no
  conception, herd and wild) would test it. It would be output-only plus a short run. That is the steward's call.

## 2. T's rise is the lower conception chance, not the gradient (moderate)
T rises against cost 0 in every 0.5 arm, as registered (prediction 1's separate clause). The flat arm shows what
drives it:
- **DEEPFLAT50 − CTLDEEP, wild T: +0.167 [+0.146, +0.189].** A lower conception chance alone raises T.
- **DEEP50 − DEEPFLAT50, wild T: −0.067 [−0.090, −0.044].** The gradient pulls T down, since T is one of the eight
  traits in p.

So the season-length mechanism I gave in 12e1e47 point 4 is real, but it belongs to the reduced conception rate, not
to the gradient on p.

**Fix:** the paper should say "T rises when conception per heat falls (flat arm +0.167); the gradient itself lowers
T", not "the per-heat cost favours a longer season".
