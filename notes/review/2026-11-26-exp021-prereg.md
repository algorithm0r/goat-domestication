# Review of the exp021 prereg, captive fear at capture (2026-11-26)

On EXPLOG "## exp021 — Does the fear a captive brings to the herd decide whether the herd closes" and its code change,
both in 1acdee5: src/params.js capturedFearSet, src/world.js capture(), and src/datamanager.js capturedFear. I read src
at HEAD, which is 1acdee5. I read no exp021 output, including the four scratch probes.

**The code change is as described.**
- capture() adds the captive's fearOfHumans to ys.capturedFear before the switch acts, then sets it if
  capturedFearSet ≥ 0 (world.js:939-940). No draw happens either way.
- The year row gives the mean per capture (datamanager.js:169).
- With the default of −1, nothing in the run changes, which the identity probes will test.

## 1. Fear of humans does rise again with alarmLearn 0 (major)
**The prereg.** "With alarmLearn 0, as in these runs, fear of humans never rises again (learnFear, animals.js:558, is
the only path up)." It goes on to say an escaped herd goat and its wild-born line stay tame.

**The code.** There are two paths up, and neither needs alarmLearn:
- **Forgetting.**
  - animals.js:555-558 is `forgetFear`, not learnFear: each day, learned fear relaxes toward 1, the naive value, at
    forgetRate 0.002 (params.js:396, "half-life ~1 year").
  - It runs on every live goat every day (world.js:1595, inside lifeHistory's loop over all goats), herd and wild
    alike.
- **Hunting.** learnFear('fearOfHumans', …) is called without the alarmLearn gate:
  - after a failed capture, with 0.5 (world.js:778, 785);
  - after a hunt that misses, with 1 (world.js:812, 845);
  - for goats that see a kill, with witnessShare 0.5 (world.js:807 to `witness`, 1945).

**What follows.**
- **The wild doesn't stay tame.** An escaped tame goat relaxes back toward naive over about a year, and is
  re-sensitised by hunters. A kid born in the wild starts at its mother's fear (animals.js:68) and relaxes the same
  way. The objection's mechanism, as written, misdescribes the model.
- **wildHerdShare doesn't measure tameness.** LOAD05's 0.844 against REF's 0.128 (EXPLOG, the exp010 flow_tab
  paragraph) is a genome share. It says nothing about whether the wild's fear is low, since fear is learned and
  passed only from mother to kid.
- **Herd adults don't settle at fearMin.** They habituate nightly toward 0.05 at habAdult 0.02 (world.js:990,
  animals.js:526-528) and forget daily toward 1 at 0.002. So they settle where the two balance, above 0.05. The prereg
  says "toward fearMin 0.05", which is the target, not where they end.

**Fix:**
- Rewrite the objection with both paths.
- P3 is still worth reporting: it is the direct test of whether LOAD05's captives arrive tamer. But its stated reason,
  "LOAD05's captives come from a tame wild", should become "if the herd-descended wild arrives tamer". It should name
  forgetting and hunting as working against that.

## 2. Tethering may absorb CAPWILD's manipulation (major)
**The prereg** names the confound: CAPTAME captives are never tethered, and CAPWILD's nearly always are. It leaves the
split for later.

**It goes further than named.**
- **Arrival fear above the threshold is erased.** isTethered is recomputed every sub-tick: R × fearOfHumans ≥
  captureFear 0.35 (animals.js:253). A tethered captive is kept at camp and is never spooked (animals.js:273-275). It
  habituates nightly (world.js:990) until R × fear drops below 0.35, and then walks. So every captive that arrives
  above the threshold joins the walking herd at about the same R × fear, just under 0.35, whatever its arrival fear
  was. Arrival fear above the threshold changes only the time spent at camp.
- **What CAPWILD can change.** It differs from LOAD05c in walking spook exposure only for the LOAD05c captives that
  would have arrived below the threshold.
  - If most LOAD05c captives arrive above it, CAPWILD barely touches the walking months H is about.
  - Then a failed P2 says nothing against H.
- **CAPTAME acts on the walking months.** It puts every captive into the walking herd at once, at fear 0.05, well
  below where tethered captives begin to walk.

**So the two arms don't test H symmetrically, and P2 may be close to void by construction.**

**Fix, before queueing:**
- **Add output fields,** output only with no draw: the share of captives with R × fear ≥ captureFear at capture,
  before and after the switch, and the days they spend tethered. Without these, the scored text can't say how far
  either manipulation reached walking captives.
- **Split the confound now, for P2 at least.** Add a pair at tetherNew 0, LOAD05c-T0 and CAPWILD-T0, and the same for
  REF if P1 is to be read on the walking months alone. tetherNew changes every herd goat's exposure, so each
  tetherNew 0 treatment needs its own tetherNew 0 baseline.
- **Cost.** This adds 16 or 32 runs. exp018's REF s1 took 9485 s and exp010's LOAD05 s1 4747 s at 4000 years.
- If the split is left for later, P2's failure has to be registered now as "not read against H", not as evidence.

## 3. The thresholds and the counting (moderate)
**The two thresholds.**
- P1 asks the R gap to move by 0.20 and P2 by 0.05, on the same scale: REF −0.636 against LOAD05 −0.104 (EXPLOG,
  the exp010 paragraphs).
- Both are well above seed noise. For comparison, REFT's SE was 0.006 (EXPLOG, the exp014 SCHEDT paragraph).
- But the prereg gives no reason for either, or for one asking four times the other. Under H in full, CAPTAME moves
  toward LOAD05's gap and CAPWILD toward REF's, so the room each side has to move is the same difference.

**Fix:**
- Set both as the same share of the REF − LOAD05 difference, or state why P2 asks less.
- csire has no margin: "higher" or "lower" by any amount passes. Give a margin, or the seed spread it must exceed.
- P1 says "6+ of 8 seeds" and P2 says "6+ of the seeds read". Say whether an unestablished seed counts against P1.

## 4. The decision rule and the "Why" claim more than P1 and P2 can show (moderate)
**The decision rule.** "P1 and P2 both hold: captive fear is a lever on closure, and closure, not the filter, separates
REF from LOAD05."

P1 and P2 can show only that captive fear at capture moves closure and the R gap. They can't show that the filter
doesn't separate the arms.

**The "Why"'s own figures show the filter differs.** "The selection is not what separates the arms" cites d7 REF T
+0.027 against LOAD05 T +0.007, so the filter is present in both but differs in size. The follow-up also couldn't
split d7 into R, fear and distance.

**Fix:**
- In the decision rule, keep "captive fear is a lever on closure and the R gap", and drop "closure, not the filter".
- In "Why", write "both arms have the filter; its size differs (d7 +0.027 against +0.007)".

## 5. The wild-fear formula: the field meanings hold (minor)
**The fields.**
- `goats` is n, every live goat at year end (datamanager.js:121, with n counted at 90).
- `learnedFear.humans` is fH / n over the same goats (150).
- `herded` is nh (151).
- `herdFearOfHumans` is fHh / nh over herd goats (93, 178).

So (goats × learnedFear.humans − herded × herdFearOfHumans) / (goats − herded) is the mean over non-herd goats at
year end.

**Caveats:**
- Both fear fields are rounded to 4 decimals before they are multiplied back. The error grows as goats / (goats −
  herded), so it matters only where the wild is small against the herd.
- "Non-herd" includes wild kids and every age.
- The derived value must come from the committed analysis tool, which the prereg already says.

## 6. The run length, the run count and the pairing (minor)
**4000 years** keeps the standing window, 3201-4000, comparable with exp010 and exp018. It makes REFc and LOAD05c a
4000-year identity check, which is stronger than the 470-year probes. exp018 and exp010 have 3-year rows (years
2-3998). Say which years the identity compares.

**Rerunning the baselines** is needed only for capturedFear (P3) and identity. The cheaper alternative is exp018 and
exp010 as baselines, with P3 read from the treatments only, but P3 needs both baselines. Keep 32, or 48 to 64 with
point 2.

**Seed pairing.** capturedFearSet makes no draw, but the runs diverge at the first capture, which is soon after
herdingAt. So "paired by seed" shares only the pre-capture years, and the pairs are effectively independent. Say so.

**The job list.** tools/exp021.jsonl doesn't exist yet. Commit it before queueing.

## 7. Wording (minor)
- **"orphans taken with their mother".** An orphan is taken when its mother is killed (world.js:811). Its capture fear
  is its own, from its mother (animals.js:68). capturedFear pools orphans with adult captures, so report them apart,
  or note it.
- **The P3 ratios.** "1.8-2.9 times" and "about 1.3" should be the printed 1.756-2.890 and 1.342 (exp020 P3).
