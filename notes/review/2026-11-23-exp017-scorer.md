## 1. A level where P1 can't be tested gets the note "not about density" (moderate, wording)
Choice 2 is sound on P1 itself: when no bin above 1-9 has 10 rows, or 1-9 has no spook, P1 fails, which is the strict
side of "in every level". But `read17` then prints the registration's failure note for that level: "that level's
reading is not about density".

That note was written for a level that showed no rise in cascade with herd size. A not-testable level showed nothing
either way: its herds never grew past 9 for 10 rows in 401-1200. The likeliest cases are SPR10 and maybe SPR05, where
the herd stays small. That is exactly where a reader would like the density question to stay open, not be closed.

**Fix:** keep P1 failing. For a not-testable level, print "density not checked at that level (its herd did not reach
a bin above 1-9 for 10 rows in 401-1200)" instead of "not about density". Keep the registered note for a level that
was tested and failed. This is a label change, and `mech17`'s text already tells the two cases apart.

## 2. Minor
- **P3 part 1 fails when no SPR level has 2 or more established.** The registration's "every level with 2 or more
  established" would be vacuously true then. The tool takes the strict side, and that is fine: P2 would have failed
  too. Say so in the results entry if it bites.
- **No floor on rows in the 1-9 bin.** That is faithful, since the 10-row condition in the registration attaches to
  the high bin. But a level whose herds establish at once can have few 1-9 rows, pooled over the 8 seeds, so its ratio
  rests on a handful of spooks. The pooled table prints rows and spooks. The results entry should quote the 1-9 bin's
  rows and spooks beside P1 at each level.
- **The spread_probe check covers the probes only.** They are 1200 years and recorded every year, so their "401-4000"
  check is the 401-1200 check again. The main runs at 4000 years are recorded every third year (runyears.mjs,
  k = ceil(years/1600)), and the two tools read rows the same way. In the main runs a row is a recorded year row, so 10
  rows span about 30 years. Nothing changes. Say "rows = recorded year rows" in the results entry.
- **The REF source.** The registration (ae6ddc4) names exp007's REF, but exp007's folder has no REF runs. The tool
  reads exp005's REF, the same eight runs as exp010's REF row. The results entry should name exp005 and say that REF's
  row reproduces exp010's (herd 287.8, R −0.636, breadth 6, csire 0.064, from exp010's scored.txt).
- **Branch 1 lists only the levels that meet it.** A level at 2-5 that would meet branch 2 isn't mentioned beside it.
  That follows the registration's "otherwise", and the trait table shows it. No change is needed.
