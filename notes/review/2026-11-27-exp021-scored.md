## 1. The headline reads P1's failure as being about closure, but the closure criterion was met (major)
**The draft:**
- The decision rule (line 86): **"learned captive fear is not what closes REF's herd"**.
- For Chris (line 119): "the fear a captive brings is not what separates REF's closed, deep herd from LOAD05's open
  one."

**What P1's cells show.** P1 joins two criteria:
- **The closure criterion, csire up by 0.05 or more, is met in 6 of 8 seeds** (capfear_tab.txt 57).
- The R gap criterion is met in none.

The other closure measures move the same way in every seed:
- depth is 10.8-12.9 against REFc's 12.5-15.8;
- wildHerdShare is 0.279-0.361 against 0.102-0.175.

So P1 fails on the R gap alone. Tame captives open REF's herd in every closure measure printed, and the R gap stays.

**What the registered sentence says.** It speaks of closure. It was written for the case where neither prediction
holds, and it can't distinguish "closure doesn't move" from "closure moves and the R gap doesn't". Here the data are
the second case. As it stands, the bold line and For Chris tell Chris the opposite of the csire line.

**Fix:**
- Keep the registered verdict and its words, labelled as the registered wording.
- Directly beside them, add: "P1 fails on the R gap criterion alone (0 of 8). The csire criterion is met in 6 of 8
  seeds, and depth and wildHerdShare move toward LOAD05c's in every seed. So captive fear at capture moves REF's
  closure measures, but not its R gap."
- **In For Chris**, replace line 119 with the same two facts. CAPTAME's herd stays far from LOAD05c's: csire 0.130
  against 0.333, depth 10.8-12.9 against 3.7-4.4. That may be said, as printed values, without "not what separates".
- **Line 72's "a little more open"** should give the values (csire 0.130 against 0.064), not the adjective.

## 2. Breadth and herd size, both registered measures, are left out (moderate)
**The prereg** lists "R gap and breadth" and "establishment and herd size" among the measures (1acdee5, Measures).

**The arm table** (capfear_tab.txt 42-45):
- **Breadth:** REFc 6, LOAD05c 1, CAPTAME 3, CAPWILD 0.
- **Herd:** REFc 287.8, LOAD05c 261.9, CAPTAME 275.3, CAPWILD 261.3.

CAPTAME's breadth, the number of core traits with an arm-mean gap of 0.10 or more, is 3 against REFc's 6, while its R
gap is −0.604 against −0.636. A reader who sees only the R gap is told that the herd's divergence didn't move. A count
that halves says it may have, in other traits.

**Caveat.** Breadth is one count per arm, taken at a threshold over arm means (load_tab.summary). It has no seed spread,
and a trait sitting near 0.10 can cross it on a small move. The per-trait gaps aren't printed.

**Fix:**
- Report breadth and herd per arm as printed, with that caveat.
- Don't read the 6-to-3 change as a finding without per-trait gaps from a committed tool. If the paper needs it, that
  is a request to the steward, not this text.
