## 1. "Where no fixed-load herd has formed" is true only at 2 and above (moderate; my miss in c2fed28)
**The text.** 53ee45e point 2, and the tool's note at line 159: an established arm "with loadMean above 1 … a herd
persists at a mean load where no fixed-load herd has formed. That is reported as the rise mattering for persistence."

**The record.** No fixed load between 1 and 2 has been run:
- LOAD1 establishes 8 of 8;
- LOAD2, LOAD3 and LOADHI establish none (EXPLOG 6111-6117);
- exp010's reading is that "the edge lies between loads 1 and 2" (6124-6125).

So at a mean load between 1 and 2, the record can't say whether a fixed-load herd would form. The persistence reading
holds only at 2 or above. I passed 53ee45e without catching this. HPH40 and HPH80 may well settle between 1 and 2.

**Fix:**
- **The steward:** a one-line note before any scored output, saying that an arm read with loadMean between 1 and 2 is
  "not matched; no fixed-load run at that load", with no persistence reading. The reading stands at 2 or above (LOAD2,
  LOAD3, LOADHI).
- **The tool:** print the two cases apart, and plant one of each in the selftest.

## 2. Q matches the established seeds' gap to all seeds' load (moderate)
**The code.** Q reads S[a]["L"], the loadMean pooled over all 8 seeds (line 157). S[a]["R"] is the R gap over the
established seeds only (306).

**The problem.** An unestablished seed's rows are a small herd at a low load. So they pull the arm's matched load down,
toward the floor, while its gap excludes them. The gap and the load it is matched at then come from different runs.

**Fix:**
- Q reads each arm's established seeds' loadMean (Le, already computed at 305), so the gap and its load cover the same
  seeds.
- Keep the all-seed loadMean for P2.
- Relabel the reading-choice line (331-333) to give the all-seed Q beside the registered one.
- With every arm read established in 8 of 8, the two coincide.
