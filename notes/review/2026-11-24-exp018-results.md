## 1. The herd stands on one cell. That is a property of the model's herding, and it sets the herd side of any nearby-goats rule (major; what goes to Chris)
**What the evidence shows.** In every per-seed line of every arm and state, with-herder own9 is own plus a few
hundredths at most. For example:
- REF s1 est: 101.337 against 101.370, the widest I found;
- SPR05 s2 est: 100.935 against 100.950;
- SPR10 s2 small: 4.098 against 4.099.

So no herd goat stands in the eight cells around a with-herder goat. The other-herd class, mostly kids following
mothers, sits on the same cell: REF est other-herd own is 101.697-114.280.

**Why the model does this.** grazeMove ranks the 3×3 by `need × forage − herdPull × (0.5 + S) × distance to the
herder` (animals.js grazeMove). Once the pull outweighs forage, the whole herd stands on the herder's cell.

The scored entry's paragraph for Chris says "the with-herder goats stand on one cell: about 100 in an established
herd". It presents this as a fact about how crowded herds live. Three things follow for Chris.
- **It may be an artefact.** A herd of about 100 grazing one cell at midday is not how a herd grazes. Whether it is
  intended is Chris's call, and it bears on more than this table: what a herd eats, and how exp017's spread alarm
  travels within a herd.
- **For a herd goat, a nearby-goats rule is the herd-load rule again.** With the herd on one cell, a spook chance that
  rises with goats in the cell rises with the herd's head count. REF's herder spook already does that: herdLoad =
  herdCount / herdPerHerder, floored at minLoad (world.js:224-227). So on the herd side, the rule Chris is weighing
  would reproduce REF's scaling rather than add something new. Only the wild side is new. That reading is mine and is
  not checked.
- **The wild side is small and narrow.** Wild own over every arm, state and seed is 1.689-1.987.

**Fix:**
- In the paragraph for Chris, say that the herd stands on the herder's cell because of how grazeMove trades forage
  against the pull, not because of a measured herd spacing, and ask whether that is intended before the table sizes a
  rule.
- Add the herd-load parallel as a reading, marked unchecked.
