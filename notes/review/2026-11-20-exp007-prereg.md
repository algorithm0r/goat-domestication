# Review of the exp007 pre-registration (2026-11-20, before results)

On EXPLOG "## exp007 — Reviewer's design checks" (e20d071; EXPLOG 5378-5411 after the exp006 correction was inserted above it) and tools/exp007.jsonl. I checked each
arm's arguments in the job list: LOADLO `herdPerHerder=1e9`; LOADHI `herdPerHerder=1e9 minLoad=4.8`; CTL arms
`cullRule=random offtakeRate=0.13 herderAlarm=0 sireRule=deep|random|shallow`; NOCULL is the bare culture with
`offtakeRate=0`. They match the entry. herdPerHerder and minLoad are read only in herdLoad (`world.js:183`), and
herderAlarm only in the spook line (`animals.js:363`). Ranked by severity.

## Major

### 1. The lineage branch of the decision rule reads a failed prediction 3 as support for isolation
**Element.** "If 3 fails (deep differs from random with no trait selected), the lineage block runs as planned with this
control added". The CTL arms are described as "a herd regime that selects on no trait by rule (the S pull of herd goats
toward the herder remains)".

**How it fails.** Two routes other than isolation can make CTLDEEP differ from CTLRAND.
- *Within-herd selection remains.* (a) Herder rules that read R are still on: tethering (`animals.js:250`, R ×
  fearOfHumans ≥ captureFear keeps a goat at camp, fed all year and protected), the night-stray approach test
  (`world.js:849`), and the capture approach test (`world.js:660, 667`). (b) Natural selection inside the herd's own
  environment continues: provisioning, penning, guarding, milking and kid shelter change what T, M, B, L, W and A are
  worth, through energetics, wolves and breeding season. Deep sires carry more generations of all of it.
- *The wild moves and the deep herd does not follow* (design review finding 2b). The two arms share one wild, so the
  wild's drift does not cancel when one herd tracks it through gene flow and the other does not.

Neither route is isolation moving the herd. As the design review and the author's response both say, isolation by
itself gives drift, which has no direction across seeds. A failed prediction 3 is therefore expected under the
"within-herd selection carried by isolation" reading too. It cannot by itself justify running B4 as planned.

**Fix.** Make the herd-change/wild-change split a registered measure for the CTL arms, not an exploratory one. It needs
the analysis tool first. Restate the branch, for example: "if CTLDEEP − CTLRAND differs beyond noise (item 2) **and**
the difference is mostly herd change, deep sires carry within-herd selection the random herd dilutes; if it is mostly
wild change, the deep herd fails to follow the wild. Either way the lineage claim is restated as selection or tracking
carried by isolation, and B4 keeps this control." Also correct the entry's description to "selects on no trait by the
spook filter or the keep value", and list the R-reading rules that remain.

### 2. Prediction 3's test fails by noise in the direction that supports the claim
**Element.** "3. CTLDEEP minus CTLRAND: within 0.10 on every core trait (seed means)", with 8 seeds per arm.

**How it fails.** Nine traits are each tested at 0.10 on differences of 8-seed means. In REF the per-seed SD of B was
about 0.30 (design review finding 9). The CTL arms remove the keep value, so B's spread may be much smaller, but nobody
knows yet. If any trait's SD is near 0.2, the SE of its difference is about 0.1 (my arithmetic: 0.2 × √(2/8)). Then one
of nine traits passing 0.10 by chance is likely. A noise failure sends the decision down the branch that keeps the
lineage block as planned.

**Fix.** Register the test as: |difference| > 0.10 **and** > 2 SE (over seeds), per trait, and compare it with the same
statistic for the neutral marker C, which the CTL arms record. If C's deep − random difference passes the same bar,
the test is too loose for these seeds. Name the traits the decision rests on in advance.

### 3. LOADHI is not in the decision rule, and LOADLO alone cannot show "R is herd size"
**Element.** The decision rule turns on prediction 1 (LOADLO) only. Prediction 2 (LOADHI) has no consequence.

**How it fails.** In LOADLO the spook hazard is lower in every herd above 5 goats per camp. If the R gap falls toward 0,
that shows the gap depends on how hard the spook filter is. It does not show that the gap depends on herd *size*,
which is finding 1's claim and the reason "est", r(herd, R gap) and the bistability are suspect. LOADHI is the arm that
separates the two: a fixed high load in small herds. LOADHI can also fail in an uninformative way. If strong spooking
keeps herds from growing past 30, its prediction 2 holds vacuously on the second clause, and the first clause (seed-mean
R gap) has no seeds to average.

**Fix.** Add LOADHI to the rule. If seeds with herds of 30-100 reach R ≤ -0.30, the R gap tracks load, not herd size,
and the observational r(herd, R gap) is load-induced. If LOADHI herds stay under 30, report that as the result: load
scaling is what lets small herds survive the filter. Pre-register how many seeds with a herd of 30 or more prediction 2
needs (at least 4, say). Add a bimodality measure for both LOAD arms (jump years by the herd-size criterion, and the
share of 100-year windows at 50-100 goats). Then question Q1 (is the bistability made by load scaling plus a fixed
capture inflow?) gets an answer from these runs.

## Minor

### 4. NOCULL has a prediction but no consequence
If NOCULL establishes in at least as many seeds as addCull (4 of 8, EXPLOG 5276), then "from the bare culture only the
threshold cull starts a herd" (outline banner) is a quota effect. The factorial's cull factor must then be threshold
against none (or carry the realized offtake), not threshold against a 13% quota. Write that in the rule. Also report
realized offtake (culled / herd, per year) for every exp007 arm and for REF and RAND, as design-review finding 6 asked.

### 5. Compare CTL arms on established seeds, and give herd size
Deep sires raise IBD, and the model has no inbreeding depression, but they may still change persistence or herd size.
If establishment counts or herd sizes differ between CTLDEEP and CTLRAND, the trait comparison is across different
herd-size distributions (design review finding 5). Register: compare established seeds only, report n per arm, and put
herd size beside each gap difference.

### 6. REF is pooled from two code versions without an identity check
LOADLO and LOADHI are compared with REF from exp005 (code cda20ea) and exp006 (v0.1.0-8/9), and exp007 runs on 0bc2e21,
which adds the sex-cull preference terms (default off). Earlier code changes in this ledger each had a cmp_years identity
check (exp004 check 1, EXPLOG 5099-5100). One scratch REF seed, 600 years, on 0bc2e21 against exp005 REF_seed1, 0
records differing, would cover it.

### 7. Report jump years, not only the end-window mean
"Established = mean herd ≥ 100 over 3201-4000" is right to use herd size. A seed that jumps at year 3900 still scores
below 100, though. Report each seed's jump year by the same herd-size criterion, censored at 4000 (design review
finding 7), so exp007 adds to the time-to-event record. Keep the criterion the same as the one the analysis session's
jump_tab.py adopts.

## On the amendment (5157d3d), before any result
The amendment answers findings 1-7. Four gaps remain, all minor.
- **An R difference in the CTL arms has no reading.** The lineage branch reads herd change "in a trait no remaining rule
  reads (any but R)" and wild change. A deep-random difference in R carried by herd change is neither. Name it, for
  example as "selection by the remaining R-reading rules (tether, stray, capture), carried by isolation".
- **"Carried by" needs a cut.** Say which reading applies when herd change and wild change both contribute (for example,
  whichever accounts for more than half of the difference, and "mixed" if neither does).
- **Established-only CTL comparison needs a minimum n.** If either arm has fewer than 4 established seeds, the 2 SE bar
  rests on 1-3 values. Say what happens then (score from all seeds with herd as a covariate, or report it as
  inconclusive).
- **NOCULL's middle case.** The rule covers 6 or more of 8. Say what 1-5 of 8 means for the cull factor (most likely:
  the quota contributes, and the factorial reports realized offtake beside the factor).
