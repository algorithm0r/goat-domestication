The contrast is what the data carries cleanly:
- **R gap,** SCHEDT − REFT: +0.387 [+0.253, +0.520].

- **Wild change,** SCHEDT − REFT: −0.075 [−0.172, +0.022], which does not exclude 0.

So most of the shortfall is in the herd, not the wild following it.

**Fix:** keep branch 2 as the registered outcome. In the entry and the paper, say what it rests on:

  in the herd's own change.

## 2. The rate difference runs against branch 2, not for it (moderate, reading)

are beyond 15%. Each run-off is a recapture attempt, and the approach test reads R and fear (`world.js:783`,

SCHEDT's recapture filter and narrow the gap difference, which is the bias I named in 3c9ab09 point 2. The contrast is
large in spite of it.

The unflagged window ratio (1.15, at the boundary) therefore doesn't threaten branch 2's direction. Nor does the
whole-history excess. This is an argument from the code path, not a measurement.

**Fix:** the entry should state the direction: "SCHEDT ran off more than REFT throughout (19 of 36 centuries beyond
15%), which gives its recapture stage more tests; the difference in R gap is in spite of this."

## 3. "Recapture stage's share of the filter, 0.41" over-attributes SCHEDT's gap to recapture (moderate, wording)

closed herd with no loss on run-off at all also opens an R gap. That is exp011 TAPER: captureTaper 1, selfDoes 10,
runOffLoss 0, the same seeds. In its scored.txt the R gap is −0.117 (SE 0.031), and in split_est the herd change of R
is −0.177.

I diffed TAPER_seed1's parameters against SCHEDT_seed1's. They differ in:

- the spook's load settings and schedule, which act only through run-offs;
- the output-only logHistDepth.

With runOffLoss 0 neither stage can select by loss. So part of SCHEDT's gap, and of REFT's, is what a closed herd does
with no run-off filter. Under the team's reading that relaxed selection is the mechanism, that part is divergence too, and I am not
asking for it to be subtracted from anything. The point is only that it isn't recapture.

**Fix:**
- Label the line "SCHEDT's R gap as a share of REFT's", not "the recapture stage's share of the filter".
- If the paper wants to say what recapture alone adds, exp011 TAPER is the nearest no-filter reference. Comparing it
