How to read this page

Each experiment runs the goat simulation under several conditions: different herding practices, or none. A condition is run several times with different random seeds, and the charts pool those runs.

Herd goats are goats a camp owns: captured from the wild, born to a herd mother, or traded in. Wild goats are all the others, including the descendants of herd goats that escaped. Where a goat is doesn't matter.

Every goat carries ten genes, each a value from 0 to 1. Each gene section shows the spread of that gene's values over time as a heatmap: time runs left to right, the gene value runs from 0 at the bottom to 1 at the top, and the brighter a cell, the larger the share of goats with that value then. The line chart beside it gives the average value, wild and herd. Hover over any chart to read its values.

Current settings means runs made with the model's standard settings from 2026-11-11 on; runs on older settings are kept for comparison and may differ in other ways.
